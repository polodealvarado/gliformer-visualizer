MEET THE MODEL

One encoder.
Several kinds of answers.

GLiFormer is Knowledgator’s framework for generalist information extraction. This walkthrough runs the 575.6 million parameter Large v1 checkpoint, a layout-aware DeBERTa model.

What does “text + image” mean here?

For a document page, a PDF text reader or OCR first finds the words and their boxes. This checkpoint reads the text and the 2D coordinates. You can see those boxes on the page and their spatial embeddings in the tower.

The page image is the visual reference. This checkpoint has no image-pixel encoder or audio head. Other GLiFormer variants support those modalities.

Read the numbers honestly

Attention shows where a token gathers information; it is not a proof of why an answer was produced. The meaning map is a 3D PCA projection of 1,024-dimensional vectors. Display strips show the first 32 dimensions. Scores are model outputs, not guarantees of correctness.

The checkpoint is documented for English. Try other languages as experiments and inspect the results.
